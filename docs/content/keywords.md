## 1. How this research was done

1. Live Google searches for every service + "Dubai" (and area, cost, "how to", disposal, donation and permit variations).
2. Review of the pages that rank: competitor service sites, Dubai Municipality (dm.gov.ae), property portals (Property Finder, Bayut, dubizzle), news (Gulf News, Khaleej Times) and classifieds.
3. Competitor structure review of the two reference sites named in the brief (keywords and topics only — **never copy wording**):
   - getjunkremovalsdubai.com — services: junk, furniture, appliance, office cleanout, garden waste, house clearance, electronic junk. ~8 area pages. Blog: water heater, wood waste, dining table disposal.
   - junkserviceindubai.com — services: junk, garbage, furniture, sofa, appliance disposal, waste, garden waste, house & villa clearance, same-day. **40+ area pages.** Blog: choosing a junk company, how waste collection works, colour-coded bins.
4. Search intent classification for every term (what the searcher actually wants).

### What this file does **not** contain

Exact monthly search volumes. The free tools used here don't expose reliable volume data, and inventing numbers would mislead. Priority below is based on **commercial intent + evidence of competition + fit with our services**.

➡️ **Action:** verify volumes in **Google Keyword Planner** (location: Dubai / UAE) and, after 4–8 weeks of traffic, in **Google Search Console → Performance → Queries**. Add a `Volume` column to the tables below when you have real data.

## 2. Key findings that shape the strategy

| Finding | What it means for us |
| --- | --- |
| Most page-one results are thin "junk removal Dubai" pages that all say the same things (same-day, eco-friendly, "80% recycled"). | We win on **specific, useful, furniture-led content** (dismantling, lifts, villas, luxury pieces) rather than generic claims. |
| **Dubai Municipality offers a free bulky-waste collection** for furniture and appliances, but **not in real-estate development zones and free zones** (source: dm.gov.ae, 2022). | Huge informational intent ("how to dispose furniture Dubai"). Our audience in master communities and free zones often **can't** use it — explain this honestly and position the private service. |
| "Used furniture buyers Dubai" is a big, crowded search (buyers who pay cash for furniture). | Different intent — searchers want to **sell**, not pay for removal. Don't target it directly; target "sell, donate or dispose" content instead. |
| "Free junk removal" searches are served by charity/resale collectors. | Don't promise "free". Address the topic in guides and explain when paid removal is the better option. |
| Competitors cover 40+ areas incl. JBR, JVT, Al Furjan, Motor City, Sports City, Town Square, Mudon, Al Barari, DAMAC Hills 2. | Area expansion is an opportunity — **only with client approval** (never Deira or Karama — brief requirement). |
| Competitors offer **garden waste removal** and **e-waste/electronics** pages. | Possible new service pages **only if the client actually provides these services**. |
| Move-out searches (permits, NOC, service lift, Ejari, DEWA) are high-volume informational topics. | Strong blog cluster that links naturally to villa clearance / furniture removal. |

### ⚠️ Brand risk — must be resolved by the client

An established company called **"Take My Junk UAE" (TakeMyJunk)** has operated across all seven emirates for ~10 years, with press coverage (Gulf News, Arab News). Our brand **"TakeJunk"** is very close.

- Branded searches like "take junk Dubai" will likely show them first.
- Customers may confuse the two businesses.
- There may be a trademark issue.

**Recommendation:** client should check trade name / trademark availability with a UAE advisor before investing heavily in branded marketing. Our non-branded keyword strategy below is unaffected.

### 3.4 Area pages `/areas/[slug]` (28 pages)

Pattern: **"furniture removal [area]"** (primary) + **"junk removal [area]"** (secondary) + the area's most relevant service (e.g. "villa clearance Arabian Ranches", "office furniture removal Business Bay").

| Area type | Add this modifier in copy | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Villa communities | villa clearance, garden furniture, garage | Arabian Ranches, Emirates Hills, The Meadows, Jumeirah Islands, Tilal Al Ghaf |
| Towers / apartments | service lift, building permit, apartment | Dubai Marina, Downtown Dubai, Dubai Creek Harbour |
| Mixed | villa *and* apartment | Palm Jumeirah, JVC, Dubai Hills Estate, DAMAC Hills |
| Business districts | office furniture removal, after-hours | Business Bay, Trade Centre / DIFC, JLT |

Use common short names searchers type, alongside the full name: **JVC**, **JLT**, **DIFC**, "the Palm", "Ranches".

### 3.5 Modifiers — use / avoid

| Use naturally | Use carefully | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| same day, near me, in [area], professional, fixed price, villa, apartment, office, eco-friendly / responsible disposal, dismantling | affordable (fine), "free" (only when explaining options, never promising free removal) | cheap (conflicts with premium positioning), "best/#1" (unprovable), "licensed/insured/certified" (only if the client confirms), "Deira", "Karama" |
